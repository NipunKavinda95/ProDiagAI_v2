# ProDiag AI

### Predictive Maintenance Intelligence for Industrial Assets

ProDiag AI is an industrial predictive maintenance platform designed to monitor machine health, detect abnormal operating conditions, diagnose potential faults, and support maintenance decisions using machine telemetry, analytics, and AI.

The project combines Industrial IoT, machine health analytics, predictive maintenance logic, RAG-based engineering knowledge, and an AI maintenance copilot into one workflow.

---

## Product Vision

ProDiag AI connects the complete maintenance decision flow:

```text
Machine Sensors
      ↓
MQTT / IIoT Telemetry
      ↓
Machine Health Analysis
      ↓
Anomaly & Fault Detection
      ↓
AI Diagnosis
      ↓
Maintenance Recommendation
      ↓
Maintenance Decision
      ↓
Work Order & Business Impact
```
